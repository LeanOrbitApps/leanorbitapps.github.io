What is a DBT diary card?

A diary card is a short daily record used alongside Dialectical Behavior Therapy. Its fields and scales vary by person and program. Use the targets and definitions you agree on with your therapist.

Four sections, one place

  • Emotions and urges: record intensity and use labels that fit your card.
  • Target behaviors: log behaviors you are working to increase or decrease.
  • Skills used: record skills you considered or practiced and their usefulness.
  • Notes: keep the context you want to return to in session.

Review before your session

The weekly overview brings emotion trends, skill use, and behavior information together. A chart records your entries; it does not diagnose a condition or explain what caused a change.

Share a PDF when you choose

Pick a single day, a week, or a custom range. Generate the PDF and choose where to save or send it. There is no clinician login or automatic upload.
