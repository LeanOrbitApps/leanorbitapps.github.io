Make your palette practical

  • Understand your season

    Discover your place in the 12-season system, with an explanation of your undertone, contrast, and the analysis confidence.

    See your selfie against different palette colors, compare shades side by side, and share a reveal card.

From selfie to color inspiration

  1. Take or choose a clear selfie in natural light, with your face and hair visible.
  2. Review the AI-sharing disclosure and agree before submitting your photo for analysis.
  3. Explore your result and use your palette as a starting point for outfits and makeup.

Lighting and camera color can affect your result. A well-lit photo without filters gives the analysis a better starting point.

Color Analysis AI: Season · iOS app by Lean Orbit

Color Analysis AI: Season is an iPhone app that uses a selfie to suggest a seasonal color palette for clothing and makeup.

How can I find a seasonal color palette from a selfie?

Take a clear selfie in natural light and submit it in Season after reviewing the AI-sharing disclosure. Explore the suggested palette and compare colors with Draping Reveal. Lighting and camera color can affect the result.

What features does the app include?

  • 12-season color analysis
  • Recommended colors and neutrals
  • Undertone, contrast, and confidence explanation
  • Side-by-side Draping Reveal

How do I download Color Analysis AI: Season?

View Color Analysis AI: Season on the App Store. Requires iOS 17.6 or later. Free to download. Full access requires a weekly subscription or lifetime purchase; see current offers in the app. Prices and offers vary by region.
