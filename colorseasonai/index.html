From selfie to color inspiration

  1. Take or choose a clear selfie in natural light, with your face and hair visible.
  2. Review the AI-sharing disclosure and agree before submitting your photo for analysis.
  3. Explore your result and use your palette as a starting point for outfits and makeup.

Lighting and camera color can affect your result. A well-lit photo without filters gives the analysis a better starting point.
