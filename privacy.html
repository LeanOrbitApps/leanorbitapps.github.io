Your data

Privacy policy

Your diary stays on your device. You decide when to export or share it.

Effective February 16, 2026 · Clarified October 21, 2026 · Lean Orbit LLC

DBT Diary Card: Skills Tracker does not collect your diary entries. No app account, developer-hosted diary storage, advertising, or in-app analytics. Apple handles purchases separately.

Diary data stored on your device

The app stores entries locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple’s local data framework. These can include emotion and urge ratings, target behaviors, skill-use records, notes, custom labels, preferences, and summaries derived from your entries.

We do not receive these records, host a diary database, or provide an app account or automatic diary sync service.

Files you choose to share

You can generate PDF reports and choose where to save or send them through the iOS share sheet. If your installed version offers additional data-export options, those files are also created on your device. We do not receive exports unless you choose to send them to us.

Once a file is shared, the recipient and the service you select handle that copy. Saving to cloud storage, sending an email, or using a messaging service can move a copy off your device. Choose an appropriate destination with your therapist when sharing sensitive information.

Offline diary use and Apple services

Daily diary tracking does not depend on a developer-hosted service. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that collect diary data.

Apple handles App Store downloads, in-app purchases, subscription management, and purchase restoration. These Apple services may use the internet and process purchase or account information under Apple’s policies. Using an external sharing destination or opening a support link may also use network access. We do not receive your payment-card details.

App lock

The optional app lock uses Apple’s authentication system. Depending on your device, authentication can use Face ID, Touch ID, or a device passcode. The app receives an authentication result; it does not receive your face or fingerprint data.

An app lock does not protect a PDF or other copy after it has been exported. Protect your device and any shared files separately.

Reminders

If you enable reminders and allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings. Consider whether reminder previews are visible on your Lock Screen.

Deleting the app and preserving records

Deleting the app removes its local diary storage. Before deleting it or changing devices, export records you want to preserve and check the backup options available in your installed version. We cannot retrieve diary entries from a developer-hosted server because we do not store them.

Any copies you exported, sent to other people, or saved through device-level backup services must be managed separately. Apple’s device backups and any sharing services you choose are governed by their own settings and policies.

Support email

If you contact support, we receive the information you send and use it to respond to your request. You do not need to include diary entries or sensitive personal details to report most issues. This correspondence is separate from the app’s diary storage.

App Store privacy information

The app’s App Store privacy label states “Data Not Collected.” The app does not include an app account, advertising, or in-app behavioral analytics. Apple’s own services and information you voluntarily send to support are separate from diary data collected through the app.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect diary information from users, including children.

This website

This policy describes the app. The pages in this app website do not embed third-party analytics scripts or remotely hosted fonts. Visiting the website can still generate ordinary hosting logs; opening the App Store, email, or another linked service is governed by that service’s policies.

Policy updates

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change. The clarification date above reflects changes to explain purchases, exports, support email, and device-level services more precisely.
