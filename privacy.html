Privacy

Your data should stay yours.

Lean Orbit builds small tools without ads, behavioural tracking, or a business model based on personal data.

Website

A quiet visit.

This site is static. It does not use advertising pixels, behavioural analytics, or a newsletter tracker. Theme preference is saved locally in your browser so the site can remember your choice.

Apps

On-device first.

Most Lean Orbit apps work without an account and keep their data on your device. Some features require a server, such as hosted AI or device sync; when they do, the individual app and its App Store listing explain what is shared and why.

Questions

Ask directly.

For a privacy question about a specific app, email support@leanorbit.app. We read every message.
