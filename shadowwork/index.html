Shadow Work Journal - Ember · iOS app by Lean Orbit

Ember is an iPhone shadow work journal for daily self-reflection, with guided prompts and the choice to keep or burn an entry.

How can I start a shadow work journal on iPhone?

Open Ember for a daily prompt, write your response, and decide whether to keep the entry or permanently burn it. It is a personal reflection tool.

What features does the app include?

  • Daily shadow work prompts
  • Private archive of kept entries
  • Deliberate burn mode
  • Optional daily reminder
