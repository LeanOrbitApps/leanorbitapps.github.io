How to play a round
1

Pick a category

Choose any category on the home screen.

2

Answer five questions

Each round is five multiple-choice questions, drawn at random from that category. A regular round has no timer, so take as long as you like.

3

Select an answer and submit

Tap an option to select it, then press Submit. You can change your mind freely up until you submit.

4

Read the explanation

Right or wrong, you get the correct answer and a short explanation of why it's correct. Tap Learn more to open the source. This is the part worth slowing down for.

5

See your score and keep the streak

After question five you get your score out of five. Play at least one round a day to keep your streak going.

The four Pro modes

Find them under Modes on the home screen. Standard owners get one free try of Speed Round, Speed Math and Advanced Mode.

⏱️

Speed Round

60 seconds, as many questions as you can. Play all your categories or pick one. A right answer scores a point, and a wrong one takes 3 seconds off the clock. At the end you see what you missed with the explanations, and those questions go into Adaptive Practice. You can share your score as an image.

🔢

Speed Math

60 seconds of mental math. Type each answer and a right one moves on by itself, with no Submit button. Choose Add & Subtract, Multiply & Divide, Mixed, or Percentages & Fractions. The problems get harder as you get faster, and they never run out.

⌨️

Advanced Mode

Ten hard questions with no multiple choice: type the answer. Capitals, accents and small spelling slips are forgiven. Stuck? Tap Show choices to answer for half a point. If a typed answer is marked wrong but you were right, tap I was right. An optional 20-second timer per question is off by default.

🔁

Adaptive Practice

Every question you miss, in any mode, comes back for review: after 1 day, then 3, 7, 14 and 30. Get it right five reviews in a row and it's learned; miss it and it starts again. Each session has ten questions: reviews that are due come first, then new questions from your two weakest categories. The difficulty follows your recent accuracy.

Frequently asked questions
I bought Spark before version 4.0. What do I get?

Pro, at no extra cost. If you paid for the app when it was a paid download, or bought the unlock in any version before 4.0, you get everything: all 11 categories and all four modes. You will never be asked to pay again.

In most cases this is automatic: open Spark 4.0 while signed in to the Apple Account you bought with, and everything is unlocked. If it isn't, tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the home screen or in Settings.

Still locked after that? Email us and we will sort it out.

What's free?

Spark is free to download, and everyone gets two free rounds of five questions in any of the five Standard categories. After that, Spark asks for a one-time purchase. There are no daily free rounds and no ads.

Is this a subscription?

No. Both purchases are one-time payments. Nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. Purchases can be shared with your family through Family Sharing and restored on your other devices with Restore Purchases.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All 2,500 questions are built into the app, so every mode works on a plane, on the subway, or anywhere without Wi-Fi.

You need a connection only to buy or restore a purchase (handled by Apple) and to open a Learn more source in your browser.

What makes this different from the other quiz apps?

Every answer is explained, with a source, so a round leaves you knowing something rather than just scored. In Pro, Adaptive Practice brings back what you missed until you know it. And there are no ads, no account, and no subscription.

A round still takes about a minute. Spark is built so the minute is worth something.

How many questions are there?

2,500: 500 in Standard (100 in each of the five categories) and 2,000 more in the Pro Library. Questions are shuffled every round, and the app avoids repeating a question you've recently seen.

Can I change my answer after I pick it?

In a regular round, yes, any time before you press Submit. Once you submit, the answer locks in and the explanation appears.

Does the app track my progress?

Yes, on your device only. The home screen shows your day streak and best score. With Pro, Settings ▸ Your progress also shows how many questions you've learned, how many are in review, your last seven days and your accuracy in each category.

There are no leaderboards and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do review reminders work?

With Pro, turn on Daily review reminder in Settings and pick a time. Spark reminds you only on days when you have reviews due. It's off by default, and iOS asks your permission the first time you turn it on.

I think one of the answers is wrong. What should I do?

Please tell us. After answering, tap Report a problem under the question. It opens an email with the question's details filled in; add what you think is wrong and send it. If Mail isn't set up, the details are copied for you to paste into an email to support@leanorbit.app.

We read every report and correct genuine errors in the next update.

I paid but the app still shows a paywall.

Tap Restore Purchases: it's at the bottom of the home screen, in Settings, and on the unlock screen. That asks Apple to re-confirm your purchase and normally fixes it immediately.

If it doesn't: check that you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with (Settings ▸ your name ▸ Media & Purchases), and that you have a connection. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account, not to the device, so a different account will not find them.

If it's still not working, email us with your Apple Account region and roughly when you purchased, and we'll help.

The app crashed or isn't behaving. What should I do?

Close and reopen the app first, then restart your device if it persists. If you're still stuck, email us with your device model and iOS version and we'll investigate.

Is Spark suitable for kids?

Yes. There's no adult content, no advertising, and no social or messaging features, and we collect no personal data from anyone.

The app offers optional one-time purchases (Standard and Pro). If you'd rather they weren't available, you can turn off in-app purchases in Settings ▸ Screen Time ▸ Content & Privacy Restrictions.
