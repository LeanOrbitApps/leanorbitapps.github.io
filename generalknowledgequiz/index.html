Pro adds four ways to play. At the heart of it is Adaptive Practice: Spark remembers what you missed and brings it back over the following month, until you know it.

Adaptive Practice

Your misses come back until you know them.

Every question you miss, in any mode, returns for review on a spaced schedule. Get it right five reviews in a row and it’s learned; miss it and it starts again. New questions come from your two weakest categories, and the difficulty follows your accuracy.

  1. 1 day
  2. 3 days
  3. 7 days
  4. 14 days
  5. 30 days
  6. Learned

Your Progress shows what you’ve learned, what’s in review, and your accuracy in every category. Optional reminders arrive only on days when reviews are due.

  • Speed RoundA 60-second speed quiz. Right answers score, wrong ones cost three seconds.
  • Speed MathType the answer and the next one appears. Problems get harder as you get faster, and never run out.
  • Advanced ModeTen hard questions with no choices. Type the answer; spelling slips are forgiven.
