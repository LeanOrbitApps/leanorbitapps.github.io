About the studio

Few pieces. Honest materials. Built to be used for years, by one person at a time.

The best apps disappear into your day. They do one thing, do it quietly, and never ask for more than you came to give.
Studio principle no. 1
The studio

Deliberately small.

Lean Orbit is an independent iOS studio. We design and ship focused apps for people who feel a little crowded by their tools and a little uneasy about where their data ends up.

One person makes most of the decisions, writes most of the code, and answers all of the email. That's not a humblebrag — it's the constraint that keeps the work honest. A studio this size can't afford to ship something nobody needs.

Nine apps are out or on their way, across productivity, mental health, finance, small business and learning. Every one of them was built on the same three rules.

01 — Lean

In the literal sense.

Most of our apps weigh less than 3 MB. They open instantly, don't chase you with notifications, and don't ship features because someone in a forum asked. Every screen has a reason to exist or it doesn't make the build.

We prefer a one-time purchase over a subscription whenever that's the honest way to charge. You should be able to buy an app, own it, and never have it hold your own data for ransom later.

02 — Intelligent

AI, but quietly.

We use AI where it earns its place. In practice that means small, focused models doing specific work: reading a brokerage screenshot so you don't retype it, scoring an interview answer against the STAR framework, telling a maple from an oak.

We're skeptical of AI that performs intelligence for the camera. The version we like is the one you barely notice — the kind that makes an app feel more thoughtful than it has any right to be.

03 — Private

On-device by default.

Wherever it's technically possible, your data stays on your device. No ads. No trackers. No behavioural analytics following you around. Most of our apps don't ask you to make an account at all, because most of them don't need one.

Some apps do need to reach a server — the ones that use a hosted model, or that sync your inventory between an iPhone and an iPad. When that's the case we say so plainly, in the app and on the App Store listing. Privacy isn't a marketing section here; it's the default we have to justify breaking.

Stay in touch

Questions, feedback, or a quiet word about what's next?

Everything that comes through gets read. No support tickets, no bots — just an inbox someone actually opens.
