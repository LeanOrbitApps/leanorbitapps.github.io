Stockroom Inventory: ReStocker

Stockroom Inventory: ReStocker · iOS app by Lean Orbit

ReStocker is an iPhone and iPad inventory app for small businesses, with barcode scanning, low-stock reorder lists, and iCloud sync.

How can I track small-business stock on iPhone?

Scan items into ReStocker, set your low-stock thresholds, and review the automatic reorder list. Store item details and use CSV import or export when moving inventory records.

What features does the app include?

  • Barcode and QR code scanning
  • Low-stock reorder lists
  • SKU, cost, photo, expiration, and location details
  • CSV import and export
  • iCloud sync across devices

How do I download Stockroom Inventory: ReStocker?

View Stockroom Inventory: ReStocker on the App Store. Requires iOS 17.6 or later. Free to download. A subscription or lifetime purchase is required for inventory features; eligible weekly subscribers can try them with a trial. Prices and offers vary by region.
