Lock Screen Sticky Notes-Loxit · iOS app by Lean Orbit

Loxit is an iPhone sticky notes app for capturing quick thoughts and reminders from the Lock Screen, Control Center, or Home Screen.

How can I capture a note from my iPhone Lock Screen?

Add Loxit to your Lock Screen controls, then use the control to capture a thought. You can also access Loxit from Control Center or by long-pressing its Home Screen icon.

What features does the app include?

  • Lock Screen capture
  • Control Center access
  • Home Screen quick action
  • Check off completed notes
