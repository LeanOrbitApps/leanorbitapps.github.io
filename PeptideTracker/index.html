Questions about PepLedger

What does a peptide tracker do?

PepLedger helps you organize the peptide protocols and schedules you enter, record events, manage vial inventory, and review your history. It does not select a protocol or recommend an amount.

Can I use PepLedger without an account?

Yes. PepLedger has no account system. Your tracking records are stored on your device. Apple handles optional Pro purchases through your Apple Account.

Can I export my peptide tracking history?

Yes. In More, choose Export dose history (CSV) to save your history, or Save complete backup (JSON) for a restorable backup. Both are available without Pro.

Does PepLedger recommend doses or treatment?

No. Amounts, concentrations, schedules, and product details are entered by you. Calculations depend on your inputs and do not determine whether a product or amount is appropriate. Discuss medical decisions with a qualified healthcare professional.
