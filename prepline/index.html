Interview Prep AI - PrepLine

Interview Prep AI - PrepLine · iOS app by Lean Orbit

PrepLine is an iPhone interview preparation app that generates practice questions from a job description and gives STAR feedback on your answers.

How can I practice interview questions for a specific job?

Paste the job description into PrepLine, answer the tailored practice questions, and use its Situation, Task, Action, and Result feedback to improve your responses.

What features does the app include?

  • Questions tailored to a job description
  • Answer scores and STAR feedback
  • Practice across multiple job targets
  • Readiness and weak-area review

How do I download Interview Prep AI - PrepLine?

View Interview Prep AI - PrepLine on the App Store. Requires iOS 18.6 or later. Free to download. Pro uses weekly or annual subscriptions; eligible weekly plans offer a trial. See current terms in the app. Prices and offers vary by region.
