PnL Trading Journal: Vault

PnL Trading Journal: Vault · iOS app by Lean Orbit

Vault is an iPhone trading journal that scans trade confirmations, logs trading emotions, and displays profit and loss on a calendar.

How can I log trades from screenshots on iPhone?

Use Vault to scan a brokerage confirmation or import a screenshot, then review the extracted trade details and tag the emotion behind the trade.

What features does the app include?

  • On-device OCR for trade confirmations
  • Ticker, direction, price, and quantity capture
  • Emotion tags for trade entries
  • Calendar view of profit and loss
