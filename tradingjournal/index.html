PnL Trading Journal: Vault

PnL Trading Journal: Vault · iOS app by Lean Orbit

Vault is an iPhone trading journal that logs trades from your brokerage screenshots, tags the emotions behind them, and shows your profit and loss on a calendar. Free to try with 2 trades.

What does Vault help me do?

Stop repeating the same trading mistakes. Vault turns each trade into a journal entry in seconds, records why you took it, and shows which habits make you money and which ones cost you.

How can I log trades from screenshots on iPhone?

  1. Open Vault. No sign-up or account is needed.
  2. Tap + and choose "Scan a Screenshot."
  3. Pick your brokerage's completed order confirmation from Photos, or take a photo of it.
  4. Check the ticker, buy or sell, price, and quantity Vault fills in, say whether the trade opened or closed a position, and tap Save.
  5. Add a tag like FOMO or Followed Plan so you remember why you took it.

Scanning works with Robinhood, Webull, Schwab, Fidelity, E*TRADE and more, and runs entirely on your iPhone. Vault imports completed stock and ETF fills; enter options and crypto trades manually.

What features does the app include?

  • On-device OCR for brokerage trade confirmations
  • Ticker, side, price, and quantity capture
  • Emotion and setup tags for every trade
  • Color-coded calendar of daily profit and loss
  • Win rate, average win and average loss for the week, month, or all time
  • CSV export for Excel and Google Sheets
  • No account and no cloud: your journal stays on your device

How much does Vault cost?

Vault is free to download, and your first 2 trades are free with every feature unlocked. To keep logging new trades, choose Weekly ($1.99/week), Monthly ($4.99/month), or Lifetime ($24.99, one-time). Your saved trades stay viewable, editable, and exportable either way. US prices; prices vary by region. Already bought Vault when it was a paid app? You keep full access at no extra cost.
