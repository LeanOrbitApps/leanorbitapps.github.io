Troubleshooting

Common Issues

1

Scanner says it can't import this screenshot

Vault only imports completed stock and ETF fills. If you see "This is an order being placed" or "This doesn't look like an order," you scanned an order ticket, a preview, or a quote page. Open the filled order from your brokerage's history and scan that instead. Options and crypto trades need to be entered manually. If the screenshot is a completed fill and still isn't read, try selecting your brokerage manually instead of Auto-Detect and make sure the image is clear and not cropped too tightly.

2

Scanner extracted the wrong price or quantity

OCR can occasionally misread numbers, especially on low-resolution screenshots or screens with complex layouts. After scanning, you always have the chance to review and correct values in the trade entry form before saving. Try rescanning with a clearer screenshot if the results are consistently off.

3

Camera permission denied

Vault needs camera access only for the screenshot scanner. When access is off, Vault shows an "Open Settings" button; you can also go to Settings → Vault → Camera and toggle it on. Choosing a screenshot from your library works without any permission, because it uses the system photo picker.

4

Trades not showing on the calendar

The calendar shows trades based on their trade date, not the date they were logged. If you log a trade today but set the date to last week, it will appear on last week's calendar cell. Navigate to the correct month using the arrow buttons to find your trade.

5

CSV export is missing trades

"Export filtered" only includes trades matching your current search and Long/Short filter. Choose "Export all" from the share menu to include your complete trade history.

6

I already paid for Vault but I see the plans screen

Tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the plans screen while connected to the internet and signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy Vault. Your full access is restored and remembered on that device. If it still doesn't work, email us with the approximate date you bought the app.
