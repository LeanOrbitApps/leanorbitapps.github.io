Privacy Policy

Headache Tracker: StillHead · Lean Orbit LLC · Effective October 15, 2026

The short version: Lean Orbit LLC does not collect, see or sell your data.

StillHead has no account, no servers of our own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your headache diary is stored on your iPhone and synced through your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access.

Location

StillHead asks for your location only to show the barometric pressure forecast where you are. Approximate location is enough, and you can decline and type a city or ZIP code instead. The coordinates are sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to get the forecast, and to Apple's geocoding service to show a place name such as "Seattle, US". StillHead does not track your location in the background and does not keep a location history.

Weather data

Pressure data comes from Apple Weather through Apple's WeatherKit service. Apple's handling of these requests is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. Weather data sources are listed at weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is turned on for StillHead, your diary is stored in the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account so it can sync across your devices and come back after you reinstall. This data is protected by Apple and only accessible to you. You can turn sync off in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud.

Notifications

Pressure-drop alerts, check-in reminders and the free-trial reminder are scheduled on your iPhone. They're optional, and you can turn them off in StillHead's Settings or in iPhone Settings › Notifications.

Widgets

The Home Screen, Lock Screen and Control Center widgets share a small amount of information with the app on your iPhone (whether a headache is in progress and when it started) so they can start and stop a headache. This never leaves your device except through your own iCloud sync.

Exports

Doctor reports (PDF) and data exports (CSV) are created on your iPhone. They go only where you choose to send them through the iOS share sheet.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or health information.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share data with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We don't require an account or an email address.

Deleting your data

  • A single headache: open it in StillHead and tap "Delete this headache".
  • Everything on your iPhone: delete the StillHead app.
  • Your iCloud copy: iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage) › StillHead › Delete Data.

Children

StillHead is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Health information

StillHead is a personal diary. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Because Lean Orbit never receives your diary, we cannot view, recover or share it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
