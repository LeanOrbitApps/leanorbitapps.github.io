Privacy Policy

Effective February 6, 2026

The short version

Wattdo doesn't collect your data. Your tasks, energy levels, and preferences stay on your device. We don't have servers, accounts, or analytics. Your business is yours.

01Information We Collect

Wattdo collects no personal information. We do not require account creation, email addresses, names, or any identifying information to use the app.

All data you enter into Wattdo — including tasks, energy levels, notes, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using Core Data and UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

02Widget & App Group Data

Wattdo uses an App Group container to share your energy level and task data between the main app and the home screen widget. This shared data remains on your device and is not accessible to other apps or transmitted externally.

03Third-Party Services

Wattdo uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting services. There are no SDKs collecting data in the background. We do not use Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, or any similar services.

04Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. Since we collect no data, there is nothing to share.

05Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes all associated data. You can also clear completed tasks from within the app's Settings screen at any time.

06Children's Privacy

Wattdo does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information from users of any age.

07Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Since we don't collect email addresses, we encourage you to review this page periodically.

08Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at andygoinc@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.
