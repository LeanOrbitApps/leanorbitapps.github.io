Troubleshooting
WIDGET

Widget not updating

If the home screen widget shows stale data, try removing and re-adding it. Also ensure Wattdo has been opened at least once after installation. iOS limits how often widgets refresh — changes should appear within 15 minutes.

WIDGET

Widget energy level doesn't match the app

As of version 1.1, the app picks up whatever the widget last set every time you open it, so the two stay in step on their own. If you are on an older version and see a mismatch, update to 1.1. Tasks you tick off in the widget are also applied to the app the next time you open it.

GENERAL

App crashes on launch

Try force-quitting the app and reopening it. If the issue persists, delete and reinstall. Note that reinstalling will remove your task data since it's stored locally. If you continue to experience issues, email us with your device model and iOS version.

Still stuck?

Send us an email at support@leanorbit.app with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots help too. We'll get back to you within 48 hours.
