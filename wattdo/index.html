Pacing To Do & Plans: Wattdo · iOS app by Lean Orbit

Wattdo is an iPhone pacing and to-do app that adjusts your task list to your current energy level.

How can I plan tasks around my energy level?

Set your current energy level in Wattdo and assign an energy cost to each task. The list hides heavier tasks when your energy is low so you can focus on tasks that fit your day.

What features does the app include?

  • High, medium, or low energy check-in
  • Energy cost for each task
  • Hide heavier tasks on low-energy days
  • Home Screen widget
