The brief

Three rules. We hold them like gravity.

Every feature, every release, every "should we ship this" argument gets checked against these three before it goes out.

01 — Lean

Every feature earns its place.

Most of our apps are under 3 MB. They open instantly, do the one thing you came for, and get out of the way. No bloat, no upsell carousel, no dark patterns.

02 — Intelligent

AI where it actually helps.

On-device OCR that reads a brokerage screenshot. Feedback that scores an interview answer. Useful work in the background — never a chatbot pretending to be your friend.

03 — Private

On-device unless we say otherwise.

Many apps work entirely on your device without an account. Apps with cloud features or analytics explain those services in their own privacy policies.
