PDF export guide

Export a DBT diary card PDF from your iPhone or iPad

Choose the dates, generate your PDF, and share or print a copy using the method you agree on with your therapist.

By Lean Orbit LLC, the app’s developer · Published August 21, 2026 · Updated October 21, 2026 · Product walkthrough

DBT Diary Card: Skills Tracker can export entries for a single day, a week, or a custom range. The screenshot below shows the date-range and export controls in the app.

1. Review the dates you want to share

Open your weekly summary and check that the entries you want to include are present. Follow your therapist’s instructions about the fields and ratings to record.

2. Open PDF export

Use the export control from the weekly summary. On the PDF Export screen, select This Week, Last Week, Single Day, or Custom Range. Review the export summary before continuing.

3. Generate and review the PDF

Tap Generate PDF. Check the dates and contents of the generated document before sharing it. The app’s PDF is a record of what you entered; confirm that the format meets your therapist’s needs.

4. Save, share, or print

Use the iOS share sheet to save to Files, send through a sharing app, or print if that option is available on your device. Sharing and printing options depend on your apps, device, and printer.

Agree on a suitable channel with your therapist. Exporting a PDF does not notify a clinician automatically, and the app does not monitor your entries or provide an urgent response.

Looking for a blank printable card? Ask your therapist for the card used in your program. The University of Washington BRTC resource page also links to diary-card examples and instructions; follow the usage terms on that site.

What does full access cost?

The app is free to download. Full access is available through a weekly subscription or a lifetime purchase; eligible weekly subscribers can start a 3-day trial. See current US prices and purchase terms, or check the offer shown in the app for your region.

What happens to the shared copy?

Diary entries are stored on your device. When you export or send a PDF, you create a separate copy. The app’s lock does not protect that file after export. Choose its destination carefully, and manage saved or shared copies separately.
