What is a DBT diary card?

A DBT diary card is a daily record used alongside Dialectical Behavior Therapy. It helps a person and their therapist notice emotions, urges, behaviors, and skills practice across the week. DBT Diary Card: Skills Tracker is a digital diary card—not a replacement for therapy or clinical advice.

How can I track DBT skills between sessions?

Record the skills you practiced and your daily emotions and behaviors in the app, then export a PDF to discuss with your therapist. The app is a self-tracking tool, not a substitute for professional care.
