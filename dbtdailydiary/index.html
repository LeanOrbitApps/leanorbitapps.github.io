What is a DBT diary card?

A DBT diary card is a daily record used alongside Dialectical Behavior Therapy. It helps a person and their therapist notice emotions, urges, behaviors, and skills practice across the week. DBT Diary Daily is a digital diary card—not a replacement for therapy or clinical advice.
