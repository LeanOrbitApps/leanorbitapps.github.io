Using the app

What does the app do?

It keeps a daily record of emotions, urges, target behaviors, notes, and DBT skills on your iPhone or iPad. Review a weekly overview and export selected dates as a PDF. It does not provide therapy or monitor entries.

How do I complete a daily card?

Open Today, choose the date, and work through Emotions & Urges, Target Behaviors, Skills Used, and Notes. Entries save as you go. Use the categories and rating definitions agreed with your therapist.

Read the daily-card guide.

Can I customize the card?

Settings includes Emotions & Urges, Target Behaviors, Skills List, and Rating Scale options. Customize labels and visible skills to fit your routine. Check the available options against the card your therapist uses.

How do I export, share, or print a PDF?

Open the weekly summary and the PDF export controls. Select This Week, Last Week, Single Day, or Custom Range, then generate the PDF. Use the iOS share sheet to save, share, or print it.

Agree on a sharing channel with your therapist. The app does not automatically send your entries. See the export guide.

Can my therapist access my entries directly?

No. There is no clinician portal, live access, or automatic monitoring. Bring your device to the appointment or share an exported PDF yourself.

Can I use the diary without internet access?

Diary entries are stored on your device, and daily tracking does not require an app account or a developer-hosted diary service. Apple handles downloads, purchases, subscriptions, and purchase restoration, which may require internet access.

How do I enable the app lock or daily reminder?

Open Settings to configure App Lock and Daily Reminder. App Lock uses the authentication options available on your device, such as Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode. Allow notifications in iOS Settings if you want reminders.

What happens if I delete the app or change devices?

Diary entries are stored locally. Do not assume they will reappear when you reinstall the app or restore a purchase. Before deleting the app or replacing your device, preserve any records you need using the export options available in your version.

Restoring a purchase restores access, not diary entries. Contact support before making a change if you are unsure how to preserve your records.

Which devices are supported?

The App Store lists iPhone with iOS 17.0 or later and iPad with iPadOS 17.0 or later. Check the listing for compatibility with your particular device and the latest version.

Purchases & trials

Is the app free, a subscription, or a one-time purchase?

It is free to download. Full access is available through a weekly subscription or a lifetime purchase. US prices checked October 21, 2026 are $3.99 per week or $24.99 once for lifetime access. Regional prices and offers may differ.

A free download does not mean all features remain free. Confirm the purchase screen before starting a plan.

How does the 3-day trial work?

Eligible subscribers can start a 3-day free trial on the weekly plan. If you do not cancel, it converts to the paid weekly subscription at the price shown when you subscribe. Trial eligibility and current terms are displayed in the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select the app’s subscription and follow Apple’s cancellation controls. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Apple’s subscription cancellation instructions.

I bought the app before version 2.0. Do I need to pay again?

No. The version 2.0 announcement preserves full access for earlier purchasers. Use the Apple Account associated with the original purchase and the app’s Restore Purchases option, if available. If access is missing, contact support with the purchase date and app version. Do not send passwords or full payment details.

How do I restore a purchase or request a refund?

Use the same Apple Account as the purchase and look for Restore Purchases on the app’s purchase screen. If access is still missing, contact support. A restored purchase does not restore diary entries.

Apple handles refund requests through reportaproblem.apple.com. Eligibility is determined by Apple.

Purchase information reviewed October 21, 2026. The current offer and terms shown in the app apply.

Getting urgent help

This app and its support inbox are not monitored crisis services. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services. Follow the safety plan you have agreed on with your care team.
