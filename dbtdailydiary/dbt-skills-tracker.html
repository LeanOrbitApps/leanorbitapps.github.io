Track skills across the four DBT modules

The app includes 30 skills grouped into mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation, and interpersonal effectiveness. Follow the skills and instructions taught in your own program.

Mindfulness
Examples include Wise Mind, Observe, and Describe.

Distress tolerance
Examples include TIPP, Self-Soothe, and Radical Acceptance.

Emotion regulation
Examples include Check the Facts, Opposite Action, and Cope Ahead.

Interpersonal effectiveness
Examples include DEAR MAN, GIVE, and FAST.

Record attempts, not just successes

Log how you used a skill and how useful it felt. The app’s skill ratings are a record of your own experience, not a clinical assessment. Use a short note to preserve anything you want help understanding.

See skill use alongside the rest of your week

The weekly overview summarizes skills by module alongside your emotion and behavior entries. You and your therapist can decide what to discuss or practice next.

Keep the record on your device

No app account is required. When you want to share, export your diary card as a PDF and choose the recipient yourself.
