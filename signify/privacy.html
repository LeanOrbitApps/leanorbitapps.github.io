Privacy Policy

Signify — Signature Creator · Last updated 12 August 2026

Signify does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your signatures and the documents you sign are created on your iPhone, stored on your iPhone, and never sent to us or to anyone else.

This policy explains what that means in detail, and what the few pieces of data the app does store on your device actually are. It applies to the Signify iOS app published by Lean Orbit.

What we collect

Nothing. Signify has no server component. We do not receive, and cannot access, your signatures, your documents, your photos, your name, your email address, your location or any identifier for you or your device.

Because of this, the app's App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

Signatures and documents

Photographs of your signature, the cleaned-up signature images extracted from them, typed signatures, PDFs you import, and the signed documents you produce are all written to Signify's own private storage area on your iPhone. Other apps cannot read them. They are not synced to iCloud by the app.

Camera

If you allow camera access, Signify uses the camera only to photograph your signature from paper. The photo is processed on-device to separate the ink from the page. It is never uploaded.

Photo library

If you choose to import a signature image from your photo library, iOS gives Signify only the specific image you picked. The app does not browse, index or read the rest of your library, and the image is never uploaded.

Passwords for locked PDFs

If you import a password-protected PDF, the password you type is used once to open the file and is never stored, logged or transmitted.

Free-allowance counters

Signify's free tier allows a fixed number of signatures and document exports per device. To count them, the app stores a small record in the iOS Keychain under com.leanorbit.SignatureESign.allowance. It contains two numbers and a short list of randomly generated identifiers for documents you have already exported. It contains no name, email address, device identifier or advertising identifier, it is not synchronised to iCloud Keychain, and it is never transmitted.

Please note that this record is not removed when you delete the app — that is how the allowance stays per-device rather than resetting on reinstall. It is removed if you erase the device.

App preferences

Signify stores small local settings on your device, such as whether you have completed the introduction and how many documents you have exported, so it knows whether to offer the App Store rating prompt. These are ordinary local app preferences and are deleted with the app.

Purchases

Signify offers an optional subscription and an optional one-time unlock. All purchases are handled by Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see or receive your payment card, billing address or Apple Account details. The app checks with Apple's StoreKit framework on your device to confirm whether a purchase is active.

Apple provides us with aggregate sales and download reports through App Store Connect. These do not identify individual customers. Apple's own handling of your data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

When you share a document

When you export or share a signed document, iOS hands the file to whichever destination you choose — Mail, Messages, Files, a cloud storage app, a printer. From that point the file is governed by the privacy policy of the service you chose. Signify has no visibility into where you sent it.

What Signify does not contain

  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad network SDKs
  • No attribution, marketing or A/B testing SDKs
  • No social media SDKs or login providers
  • No third-party purchase or subscription platform
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track

Children

Signify is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to look up, hand over, correct or erase. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

To delete the content held on your device, delete the app; your signatures and documents are removed with it. Within the app, Settings also offers "Delete stored source photos", which discards the original photographs while keeping the signatures made from them.

Data security

Your signatures and documents are protected by your iPhone's own storage encryption and by iOS app sandboxing, which prevents other apps from reading Signify's files. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no network transfer to intercept and no database of ours to breach.
