Signify Support

Signature Creator for iPhone · Lean Orbit

Getting started

How do I make a signature?

Sign your name on a blank sheet of paper, then photograph it with Signify. The app separates the ink from the paper and gives you a clean, transparent signature. You can also import a photo you already have, or type a signature and pick a handwriting style.

How do I sign a document?

Import a PDF — from Files, from another app's share sheet, or from an email attachment — then place your signature where it belongs and export. The signed file is a new PDF; your original is never altered.

Can I sign something that isn't a PDF?

Not in this version. Document import accepts PDFs only. Support for signing images is planned.

My PDF is password-protected.

Signify will ask for the password when you import it. The password is used once to open the file and is never saved.

Free use and purchases

How does the free version work?

You get a fixed number of signature creations and a fixed number of document exports, free, on each device. Exports are full quality and are never watermarked. The counts are shown in the app under Settings.

Do the free uses reset?

No. There is no weekly or monthly reset, and deleting and reinstalling the app does not restore them. It is a one-time evaluation of the whole app, not a recurring meter. Re-exporting a document you have already exported never uses another one.

What happens when I run out?

You'll be shown the two purchase options — a weekly subscription or a one-time permanent unlock — with the price of each. Everything you made before that point stays yours: your signatures, your saved documents and every file you already exported remain fully available whether or not you buy.

I bought Signify but it isn't unlocked.

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. Purchases work on every device signed in to that account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app: Settings → Manage Subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Signify → Cancel Subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the current period ends.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something went wrong with the app itself, email us too — we'd like to know.

Privacy and your files

Where are my signatures and documents stored?

On your iPhone, inside the app's own private storage. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account and there is no server. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Deleting the app will delete my documents. How do I keep them?

Export or share anything you want to keep before deleting the app — send it to Files, iCloud Drive, or your email. Signify has no backup of its own to restore from.

Can I delete the original photos of my signature?

Yes. Settings → Delete stored source photos discards the original photographs while keeping the signatures made from them. The originals are kept only to drive the before/after comparison view.

Troubleshooting

The camera screen says access is off.

Open the iPhone Settings app → Signify → turn on Camera. You can also import a photo of your signature from your photo library instead, or type a signature — neither needs the camera.

The extracted signature looks faint or patchy.

The app works best with dark ink on plain white or light paper, photographed in even light with no shadow across the page. Avoid glossy paper and direct glare. You can adjust the result after extraction if it needs a nudge.

Signify warned me about the exported file.

Some PDFs contain elements — fillable form fields, annotations — that cannot be preserved exactly when the file is flattened with a signature on it. The warning tells you what would change before you export, so you can decide.

Does Signify run on iPad?

It runs on iPad in iPhone compatibility mode. A layout designed for the iPad screen is planned for a future update.

What does Signify require?

iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
